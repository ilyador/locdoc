Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one proposed PWA app icon for Locdoc, a local-first Linux-friendly Markdown editor. Generate ONE standalone square icon, not a board. Flat graphic design with bold simple geometry, a distinctive silhouette, a maximum of four dominant colors, crisp edges, deliberate negative space. Fun, witty, developer-culture personality; professionally drawn, not a generic cute sticker. Symbol should occupy about 72% of the square and stay inside a circular mask-safe central area. The background fills the entire square; do not bake a rounded-square boundary into the image. Must remain understandable at 32px. No text, no brand names, no lettering, no labels, no watermark, no photo, no mockup, no gradients, no glossy 3D, no tiny decorative detail. Original mark, not a literal copy of any Linux distribution logo. Concept 3, Root Hash: an expressive chunky hash/pound symbol (#) made from two vertical paper ribbons and two crossbars, joining the Unix root prompt and Markdown headings. The hash tilts very slightly forward like a character with attitude. Paper ribbons have two simple folded ends, one coral face and one warm cream face, with tiny dark fold facets. Bold abstract typographic symbol; the hash is the only permitted glyph, not accompanying text. No face, no character, no terminal window. Solid deep ink-blue background. A strong grown-up alternative to mascots, playful folded-paper geometry and excellent small-size recognition.
