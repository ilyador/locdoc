No text, no brand names, no lettering, no labels, no watermark, no photo, no mockup, no gradients, no glossy 3D, no tiny decorative detail. Original mark, not a literal copy of any Linux distribution logo. Concept 5, Dotfile Ghost: an original small white paper ghost, a playful reference to hidden Unix dotfiles.
