Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one proposed PWA app icon for Locdoc, a local-first Linux-friendly Markdown editor. Generate ONE standalone square icon, not a board. Flat graphic design with bold simple geometry, a distinctive silhouette, a maximum of four dominant colors, crisp edges, deliberate negative space. Fun, witty, developer-culture personality; professionally drawn, not a generic cute sticker. Symbol should occupy about 72% of the square and stay inside a circular mask-safe central area. The background fills the entire square; do not bake a rounded-square boundary into the image. Must remain understandable at 32px. No text, no brand names, no lettering, no labels, no watermark, no photo, no mockup, no gradients, no glossy 3D, no tiny decorative detail. Original mark, not a literal copy of any Linux distribution logo. Concept 4, Floppy Buddy: a friendly chunky retro 3.5-inch floppy-disk mascot, a nod to the local-file world and old Linux desktops. Oversized cream metal shutter at top, dark indigo disk body with one clipped upper corner, two big simple coral square eyes on a clean cream lower label, and a short dark underscore-like smile. No arms, legs, text, screws, extra objects, or small details. A bold compact pixel-adjacent flat symbol with geometric charm, not pixel noise. Solid dusty lilac background. The disk should feel like a tiny reliable local-files friend, immediately recognizable at app-icon size. Strict exclusions: no penguins, no Tux, no GNU animals, no actual Linux, distribution, or existing open-source project logos or mascots.
